TTI Annual Review 2022

Designed as a slide-scrolling website, the 2022 Annual Review showcases the company’s key accomplishments over the year.

2022review.ttigroup.com ↗
Output Type
Website · UI Design
Timeline
Oct 2022 – Mar 2023
My Role
UX/UI Designer
Tool Used

Layout & Interaction

A slide-scrolling interaction was applied to create a sense of progression across sections.

Modular layouts allowed flexibility when presenting different types of content, from statistics to brand showcases. Transitions were kept subtle to support the content rather than distract from it.

Content & Data

Content hierarchy was structured so key messages stand out within a dense amount of information. Visual direction stayed close to the brand identity while improving readability and the digital experience.

Multiple layout explorations were tested to find the right balance between corporate tone and modern interaction.

Refinement & Impact

Attention to detail was applied across components to ensure consistency and usability. Each screen was refined to stay clear across resolutions, and micro-adjustments in spacing and typography improved overall readability.

The final design communicates corporate performance in a more engaging way, turning static reporting into an interactive storytelling journey.
